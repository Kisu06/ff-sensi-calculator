About FF SENSI AI

Welcome to FF SENSI AI, a dedicated online utility tool engineered to provide gamers with mathematically calibrated sensitivity values, recommended DPI multipliers, and custom HUD fire button placement ratios for Free Fire and Free Fire MAX.

Our Mission

Our goal is to help mobile gamers achieve optimal touch responsiveness across all smartphone models—ranging from budget 2GB RAM devices up to flagship 120Hz/144Hz displays. We eliminate trial-and-error by computing parameters based on screen hardware touch sampling rates.

100% Policy Safe & Transparent

FF SENSI AI operates strictly within official gaming guidelines. All recommendations refer solely to native in-game settings menus and Android Developer Options DPI configurations.
