About FF SENSI AI

Engineering mathematical sensitivity algorithms and display calibration utilities for mobile FPS gamers worldwide.

Our Mission

FF SENSI AI was created to solve a universal challenge faced by mobile esports players: finding optimal in-game sensitivity sliders and Developer Options DPI without resorting to tedious, time-consuming trial-and-error.

Scientific Calibration Approach

Unlike generic gaming blogs that provide static, fixed numbers, our engine evaluates actual display hardware specifications—including touch sampling rates, screen refresh rates (Hz), and RAM capacities—to generate customized settings for every phone model.
