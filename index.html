About Viral Sensi Generator

Viral Sensi is an independent web utility designed for mobile gamers looking to find their ideal in-game sensitivity settings. Modern mobile gaming requires balanced settings between screen touch response, refresh rate, and camera speed.

Our generator calculates recommended values for General sensitivity, Red Dot, 2x Scope, 4x Scope, Sniper Scope, DPI, and fire button size—helping players improve their drag headshots and accuracy across all Android and iOS devices.

How Sensitivity Values Are Calculated

The Viral Sensi generator uses three core factors to calculate balanced settings:

  • Display Hardware Metrics: Analyzes screen refresh rate (60Hz, 90Hz, 120Hz) and stock display width (dp) to establish baseline touch response multipliers.
  • Combat Playstyle Focus: Adjusts General and Red Dot values depending on whether you focus on One-Tap Shotgun shots, long-range drag elevation, or rapid gloo wall drops.
  • Scope Stability: Fine-tunes 2x, 4x, and Sniper scope tracking sensitivity to prevent crosshair shake during medium and long-range combat.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Does Viral Sensi require any third-party app or config file?
No! Viral Sensi is strictly an informational manual settings generator. You do not need any third-party apps, config files, or script modifications.
2. Is using these sensitivity settings safe for gaming accounts?
Yes, 100%! All values are standard in-game menu settings provided natively inside mobile games. It is completely safe and adheres to Fair Play rules.
3. What is the best General Sensitivity for 4GB RAM phones?
For 4GB RAM Android smartphones, the optimal manual sensitivity settings are General: 160-195, Red Dot: 158-190, 2x Scope: 150-180, 4x Scope: 138-170, with a Fire Button Size of 42-44% and DPI of 480.
4. How to calculate sensitivity for drag headshots?
Select your device specifications and playstyle in our Viral Sensi generator. The tool calculates optimal drag shot sensitivity based on your phone display and touch sampling rate.
5. What is the ideal Fire Button Size for headshots?
Keeping your fire button size between 40% to 46% placed at the bottom third of your screen provides maximum thumb drag distance for clean drag headshots.
6. Is modifying DPI safe on Android?
Yes, modifying phone Developer Options DPI is a native feature on Android devices and is 100% legal and safe under developer policies.
7. How to apply these sensitivity settings in-game?
Calculate your values using the tool above, then open Free Fire, navigate to Settings ➔ Sensitivity, and manually adjust each slider (General, Red Dot, 2x Scope, 4x Scope, Sniper Scope) to match the calculated numbers. Never use any third-party panels, apps, or files; all adjustments must be done manually inside the official in-game settings.
8. What is the difference between General and Red Dot sensitivity?
General sensitivity controls primary camera rotation and un-scoped drag elevation speed. Red Dot controls target tracking speed when aiming through unmagnified sight attachments.
