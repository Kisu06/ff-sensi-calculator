Why Fire Button Placement Matters for Headshots

The size and position of your right fire button directly impact your vertical thumb drag distance. A fire button that is too large reduces drag room, causing drag shots to stop at the enemy chest rather than elevating to the head.

Optimal Fire Button Size Guidelines

  • One Tap Shotgun Focus: 38% - 42% (Provides maximum vertical drag room).
  • SMG / AR Drag Shot Focus: 42% - 46% (Provides balanced touch surface and drag room).
