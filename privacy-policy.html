Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. How we protect user data and maintain 100% compliance with Google AdSense Policies.

1. Introduction & Overview

At Viral Sensi (accessible at viralsensi.takiff.online, part of the takiff.online network), the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by Viral Sensi and how we use, safeguard, and disclose that information in full compliance with Google AdSense Publisher Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Viral Sensi is a client-side gaming utility that operates without requiring user registration, account sign-in, or payment credentials. We do NOT collect or store:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, or phone number.
  • Gaming account passwords, Free Fire UID credentials, or social media login tokens.
  • Financial, debit/credit card, or banking transaction data.

3. Log Files & Standard Web Diagnostics

Like most standard web servers, Viral Sensi uses log files. These files merely log visitors when they access the website—a standard procedure for hosting services and analytics. The information collected by log files includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized where applicable).
  • Browser brand, version, and operating system (Android, iOS, Windows, macOS).
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP) and general regional geolocation.
  • Date and time stamps of site visits.
  • Referring and exit pages, along with aggregate click counts.

This information is used strictly to analyze user trends, maintain server security, administer the site, track user movement in the aggregate, and gather demographic information for performance optimization.

Google is a third-party vendor on our website. Google uses cookies, specifically the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve targeted advertisements to our site visitors based on their visit to viralsensi.takiff.online and other sites across the Internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its certified advertising partners to serve ads based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the official Google Ads Settings page.
  • Alternatively, users can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

7. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have specific rights regarding their personal data:

  • Right to Know: The right to request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal data that a business has collected about consumers.
  • Right to Delete: The right to request that a business delete any personal data collected about the consumer.
  • Right to Opt-Out: The right to direct a business that sells personal data not to sell the consumer's personal data. Viral Sensi does not sell, trade, or monetize any user personal data.

If you make a request regarding your CCPA rights, we have one month to respond to you. Please contact us at support@takiff.online.

8. GDPR Data Protection Rights

We want to ensure you are fully aware of all your data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Every user is entitled to:

  • The Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The Right to Rectification: You have the right to request correction of any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The Right to Erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • The Right to Data Portability: You have the right to request the transfer of collected data to another organization.

9. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)
