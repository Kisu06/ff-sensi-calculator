How DPI Affects Drag Shots in Free Fire

DPI (Dots Per Inch) in Android Developer Options adjusts the minimum width (dp) of your smartphone display. Increasing your DPI reduces UI scaling, allowing your finger swipe distance to translate into faster camera rotation in Free Fire.

Safe DPI Guidelines for Android Smartphones

  • Budget 360 dp Phones: Recommended DPI range between 460 - 520.
  • Mid-Range 392 dp Phones: Recommended DPI range between 480 - 540.
  • Flagship 411 dp Phones: Recommended DPI range between 420 - 480.
